FontSwitcher keeps your font files in a library and activates them only when you need them. Font files stay where they are; nothing is copied or moved.

## How to Use
### Adding Fonts
1. Drop font files, or folders that contain fonts, onto the window. You can also choose them with "Add…" (⌘O).
2. Folders are searched including subfolders for .otf, .ttf, .ttc, .otc, .dfont, .woff and .woff2 files.
- Font files are never copied or moved. If you move a file after adding it, it appears under "Missing".
- A file at the same location is not added twice.

### Watch Folders
- Add as many folders as you like under Watch Folders in the Fonts settings. When new font files appear in a folder (including subfolders), they are added to the library without being activated.
- The folders are also read at launch. Files removed from a folder appear in "Missing".

### Activating and Deactivating
- Click the checkbox at the left of a family to activate or deactivate it. Double-clicking also toggles it.
- Families with two or more files have a ▶ at the left. Expand it to list each style (file), and use its checkbox to switch only that style. A family with only some styles active shows “–”. Files such as .ttc that hold several styles switch as one file.
- Select several families and click "Activate" (⇧⌘A) or "Deactivate" (⇧⌘D) to switch them together.
- You can also press Space with rows selected. If everything selected is active it is deactivated; otherwise it is activated.
- Activated fonts are available right away in other apps such as Illustrator, InDesign and Word. Some apps take a moment to update their font menu or need to be restarted.
- Activation lasts for the current login session. Fonts stay active after you quit FontSwitcher and are deactivated when you log out. At the next launch, fonts that were active last time are reactivated (you can change this in Settings).

### List and Preview
- Use the sidebar to switch between All Fonts, Active, Inactive, Duplicates, Missing and your sets.
- Duplicates are fonts with the same PostScript name registered more than once. Only one of them can be active at a time.
- Select one family to see every style on the right, with the format, number of characters (glyphs), version, PostScript name and location. Fonts that are not active can be previewed too.
- Change the preview text in the field at the top and the size with the slider.
- Choose the view in the menu on the left: One Line (cut off at the edge), Multiline (wrapped) or Waterfall (one line at each size from 72 to 9 pt).
- The button on the right (slider icon) opens the preview options: colors (Black on White, White on Black, or Custom, where you choose the text and background colors), opacity, kerning (use the font's kerning), tracking (1/1000 em) and preset preview texts (Lorem ipsum, letters, figures, hiragana, katakana, kanji and so on).
- Use "Localized / PostScript name / Both" above the list to choose how font names are shown. Fonts without a localized name use their English name. With PostScript names, family rows show the English family name and style rows show PostScript names.
- The "Search Options" button to the right of the search field opens a panel of filters.
  - Character set: Std, Pro, Pr6N and so on in the family name
  - Format: OpenType (CFF), TrueType, collections and so on
  - Kind: variable, color, kana or icon fonts
  - Styles in Family: the number of weights and styles in the family (1, 2–4, 5–9, 10 or more), counted within the sidebar selection
  - N (JIS2004): whether the character set name ends in N (StdN, ProN, Pr6N, PlusN)
  - UD: whether a Japanese font has UD in its name
  - AP Version: whether a Morisawa A-OTF font is the proportional A P-OTF
  - Shin Go: Shin Go or Shin Go NT
  - Characters: the number of characters in the font (up to 999, 1,000–4,999, 5,000–9,999, 10,000–14,999, 15,000 or more)
- The button is filled while a filter is on. Filters combine with the sidebar and the search; "Clear All" removes them.
- In the preview, characters the font lacks (such as Japanese in a Latin font) are shown faintly in another font.
- The button at the upper right of the list (⌥⌘P) hides or shows the preview on the right.
- Click a sidebar heading (Library, System and so on) to collapse or expand it. Collections start collapsed.
- The "…" button at the top of the sidebar chooses which sections (System, Kinds, Vendors, Collections, Smart Sets, Projects, Sets) appear and in what order. "Library" always comes first.
- The ⚠️ mark means the file is missing, or a font with the same name is in use from another location (installed by the system or another app). Hold the pointer over the mark to see why.

### Smart Sets
- "Smart Sets" in the sidebar lists sets whose contents are defined by conditions: Favorites, Active, Inactive, Recently Activated (activated in the last 7 days and still active), Recently Deactivated (deactivated in the last 7 days and still inactive), Recently Added (in the last 7 days) and Supports Japanese (fonts with hiragana and katakana).

### Projects
- A project is a group of fonts for one job, separate from sets. Choose "New Project from Document…" under "+" at the bottom of the sidebar or in the File menu and pick an IDML, INDD, PDF or AI file (saved with PDF compatibility), or drop the document on the main window; FontSwitcher checks the fonts it uses. "New Project from Font Names…" creates one from names pasted one per line.
- A confirmation window opens first. Fonts whose PostScript names match are added; candidates whose names differ slightly are added only when checked. Fonts not in the library are kept by name ("Fonts Not in Library…" in the right-click menu adds them back once they are in the library).
- The checkbox to the left of a project activates or deactivates its fonts. Activating a project does not deactivate other fonts. If something fails partway, the fonts activated in that operation are deactivated again.
- Projects are also listed at the top of the menu bar icon's menu (when shown). Choosing one activates it (or deactivates it when all of its fonts are active).
- For INDD the font list recorded in the file is used (every style of each family in use), so it is longer than for IDML. Export IDML to get only the styles in use.
- For IDML only the styles in use are read; for PDF the embedded fonts; for AI also the font list recorded in the file.

### Sets
- The checkbox to the left of a set activates or deactivates all of its fonts (it shows "−" when only some are active).
- Create a set with "+" or "New Set…" (⇧⌘N). Families selected in the list are put in the new set.
- Right-click families and choose "Add to Set" to add them to an existing set.
- Right-click a set to activate or deactivate all of it, rename it or delete it. Deleting a set keeps the fonts in the library.

### Favorites
- Click the ☆ to the right of a family name to add it to Favorites (click again to remove it). Right-click and choose "Toggle Favorite" to switch several at once.
- "Favorites" in the sidebar lists only favorite families. Favorites are remembered by family name, so they remain even if you replace the files.

### System Fonts
- "System" in the sidebar shows the three macOS font folders. They are read at launch and whenever fonts change.
- **Your user name** (~/Library/Fonts): fonts for this user only, including Morisawa fonts and the like.
- **All Users** (/Library/Fonts): fonts for every user of this Mac.
- **System** (/System/Library/Fonts): macOS fonts. They cannot be switched or removed from the library.
- Fonts under your user name and All Users can be switched with their checkboxes. The files stay in place and macOS remembers that they are off (they stay off after you log out). Check them again to turn them back on.
- To leave them out of the list, turn off "Include fonts in the font folders" in the Fonts settings.

### Kinds
- "Kinds" in the sidebar shows only variable fonts (whose weight and other axes vary continuously), color fonts (with colored glyphs such as emoji), kana fonts (with kana and almost no kanji) or icon fonts (with half or more of their characters in the Private Use Area and no extended Latin letters, fonts classed as symbol fonts, or names containing Awesome, Icon, Dingbat, Ornament, Symbols, Emoji and so on), identified by the tables in the font (fvar; COLR, sbix, CBDT, SVG).

### Vendors and Collections
- "Vendors" in the sidebar shows only Morisawa, Fontworks or Adobe fonts, identified by the trademark owner written in the font (such as "… is a trademark of Morisawa …"), or by the vendor ID (MRSW, FWKS, ADBE) when no owner is written. Morisawa typefaces built by Adobe (A-OTF Ryumin and so on) go to Morisawa. "Adobe" shows only Ten Mincho (including Text and Antique), Kozuka Mincho, Kozuka Gothic, Source Han Sans, Source Han Serif, Higumin, Momochidori, Adobe Clean, Myriad, Minion, Ryo, Source Code Pro and Noto.
- "Adobe Fonts" under Vendors shows the fonts activated in Creative Cloud. They are turned on and off in Creative Cloud, so FontSwitcher cannot switch them (Creative Cloud registers them again right away). While they are shown, "Open Creative Cloud" and "fonts.adobe.com" above the list open the places to switch them (also from the right-click menu of the "Adobe Fonts" row).
- "Collections" in the sidebar lists the collections of Font Book and the Fonts panel. Choose one to see the library fonts in it. They are read again when you bring FontSwitcher to the front.
- Right-click families or styles and choose "Add to Collection" to put them in a collection used by Font Book and the Fonts panel (~/Library/FontCollections). "New Collection…" creates a new one.
- Fonts are added by PostScript name. Inactive fonts can be added too and appear in the collection when you activate them.

### Sharing with Other Macs
1. In the Fonts settings, click "Choose Shared Folder…" and choose a folder, for example in Dropbox.
2. "FontSwitcher-Library.json" is placed in that folder and holds the fonts, sets, favorites and activation.
3. Choose the same folder on your other Mac; its library is combined so both Macs have the same contents.
- Locations inside Dropbox and your home folder are translated to each Mac's location. Files that are not on a Mac appear under "Missing".
- Changes made on another Mac are loaded when the file syncs or when you bring FontSwitcher to the front. Fonts activated there are activated on this Mac too.
- If both Macs change it at the same time, the last one saved remains.
- "Stop Sharing" keeps the current contents as this Mac's own library.

### Removing from the Library
- Select families and choose "Remove from Library…" (⌘⌫). Active fonts are deactivated first. The font files are not deleted.

### Unify Fonts in Illustrator
- "Unify Fonts in Illustrator…" in the Tools menu replaces the fonts in the front document of the running Illustrator with other variants of the same typeface (character set, N, Shin Go / Shin Go NT, UD, AP version). The conversion is the same as the AiFontConverter script.
- Choose the scope: selection, whole document, or active artboard. "Max" and "MaxN" choose the richest character set (Pr6 / Pr6N) with UD, P and NT on.
- OK checks the fonts in use and lists the changes; Run replaces them. When the same weight is missing, the nearest weight is used; fonts that are not installed are left as they are.
- The first time, macOS asks for permission to control Illustrator. You can change it in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Automation.

## Settings
Open Settings with the Settings button in the main window (⌘,).
- **Launch & Background**: Open at login, Hide from Dock and App Switcher, Show in menu bar. When "Hide from Dock and App Switcher" is on, the icon does not appear in the Dock or in ⌘Tab. Show the window from the menu bar icon (when shown) or by opening the app again in Finder. The app shortcut (⌃⇧⌘F by default) opens the main window from any app while FontSwitcher is running. When another of these apps uses the same key only for a certain front app and is running, the key is left to it while that app is in front
- **Fonts**: Reactivate fonts from last time at launch (on by default), deactivate all fonts when quitting (off by default), Library Sharing, include fonts in the font folders (on by default), and the preview text. To reactivate fonts every time you log in, also turn on Open at login.
- **Sharing**: Export and import the Fonts settings, and sync them with other Macs through a folder in iCloud Drive, Dropbox and so on. Share the library, sets and favorites with Library Sharing in the Fonts settings.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Keys for menu items (Add Fonts, New Set, New Project, Activate, Deactivate, Show in Finder, Remove from Library, Hide or Show Preview).
- **About**: Version and links.

## Hotkeys
- **⌘O**: Add Fonts
- **⇧⌘N**: New Set
- **⇧⌘A**: Activate
- **⇧⌘D**: Deactivate
- **⌘R**: Show in Finder
- **⌥⌘P**: Hide or show the preview
- **⌘⌫**: Remove from Library
- The keys above can be changed under Keyboard Shortcuts in Settings (the defaults are shown).
- **⌃⇧⌘F**: Open Main Window (from any app; change or turn it off under Launch & Background in Settings)
- **⌘0**: Open Main Window
- **⌘,**: Settings
- **⌘W**: Close Window
- **⌘?**: FontSwitcher Help
- **⌘Q**: Quit FontSwitcher

## Permissions and Privacy
- No Accessibility permission is needed.
- Only "Unify Fonts in Illustrator" needs permission to control Illustrator (Automation).
- The app connects to the network only for "Check for Updates…". The font list is saved on this Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/FontSwitcher/Library.json.
- When you add fonts from Documents or Downloads, macOS may ask for permission to access the folder.
- Use each font according to its license.

## Launching and Quitting
- Closing the window does not quit the app. Click the Dock icon or press ⌘0 to show the main window again.
- With "Show in menu bar" on, you can also choose Open Main Window, Settings… and Help from the menu bar icon.
- To quit, choose "Quit FontSwitcher" (⌘Q) in the app menu. If "Deactivate all fonts when quitting" is off, activated fonts remain usable until you log out.
- Check for new versions with "Check for Updates…" in the app menu. Until distribution is ready, it shows that updates are being prepared and makes no network connection.

## Troubleshooting
- "A font with the same name is already in use" means a font with the same PostScript name is active from the system or another font manager. Deactivate it there, then activate again.
- If an activated font does not appear in an app's font menu, restart that app.
- Fonts under "Missing" were moved or deleted, or are on a disconnected external disk. Connect the disk, or remove them from the library and add them again from the new location.
- System fonts such as those in /System/Library/Fonts cannot be deactivated.

## Help and the note Article
- Open this help with "FontSwitcher Help" (⌘?) in the Help menu.
- "Open the note Article" in the Help menu opens the article on note in your browser.

## Removing the App
1. Turn off "Open at login".
2. Deactivate all fonts if needed.
3. Choose "Quit FontSwitcher".
4. Move FontSwitcher to the Trash. The library list stays in ~/Library/Application Support/FontSwitcher; delete it if you no longer need it. Font files stay where they are.

Opening or reopening the app manually shows the main window. Automatic login launch does not show it (fonts that were active last time are reactivated).
