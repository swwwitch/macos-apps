BundleIDInspector looks up the Bundle ID of apps, lists them and lets you copy them.

## How to Use
### Add Apps
1. Drop apps on the main window, or choose File > Choose Apps… (⌘O). You can add several apps at once.
2. The list shows each app's name, Bundle ID and version (build).

### Other Ways to Add
- Drop apps on the BundleIDInspector icon in the Dock.
- Adding the same app again moves it to the top of the list (no duplicates).
- Files and folders without a Bundle ID are not added; the reason appears at the bottom of the window.

## Copying and the List
- Click Copy in a row to copy that app's Bundle ID.
- Copy All copies every Bundle ID in the list, one per line.
- Right-click a row for **Copy Bundle ID**, **Copy Path**, **Show in Finder** and **Remove from List**.
- You can also select and copy the Bundle ID text.
- Clear empties the list. The list is cleared when you quit the app.

## Settings
Open with the Settings button in the main window (⌘,).
- **Startup & Background**: Open at login, Hide from Dock and App Switcher, Show in menu bar. With Hide from Dock and App Switcher on, the app appears in the Dock and ⌘Tab while main/settings windows exist, including minimized windows, and hides after all such windows close; show it from the menu bar icon (when shown) or by opening the app again in Finder

## Shortcuts
- **⌘O**: Choose Apps
- **⌘0**: Open Main Window
- **⌘,**: Settings
- **⌘W**: Close Window
- **⌘?**: BundleIDInspector Help
- **⌘Q**: Quit BundleIDInspector

## Permissions
- No Accessibility or other permissions are required.
- The app only reads each app's Info.plist. It never changes or launches the apps and makes no network connections.

## Launching and Quitting
- Closing the window does not quit the app. Click the Dock icon or press ⌘0 to show the main window again.
- With Show in menu bar turned on, the menu bar icon also offers Open Main Window, Settings… and Help.
- To quit, choose Quit BundleIDInspector (⌘Q) in the app menu.
- Choose Check for Updates… in the app menu to look for a new version. Until distribution is set up, it reports that updates are being prepared and makes no network connection.

## Troubleshooting
- If an item is not added, make sure it is an app (.app) or another bundle. The reason appears at the bottom of the window.
- Names appear in English when the app has no localized display name for your language.

## Help and the note Article
- Open this help with BundleIDInspector Help (⌘?) in the Help menu.
- Open the note Article in the Help menu opens the article in your browser.

## Uninstalling
1. Turn off Open at login.
2. Choose Quit BundleIDInspector.
3. Move BundleIDInspector to the Trash.

Opening or reopening the app manually shows the main window. Automatic login launch does not show it. Explicitly opening files takes precedence.


## Dock visibility and application menus

Even with Hide from Dock and App Switcher on, a main or settings window keeps the app in the Dock and ⌘Tab so its application menus remain available. Minimized windows stay listed. Close all such windows to hide the app; palettes alone do not show it. Your saved setting is preserved.

Selecting the app in the ⌘Tab application switcher shows its main window and restores it if minimized. Automatic login launch stays quiet.
